# EcoWing Interface Guidance

## Design Context

EcoWing combines a mission-led environmental website with a practical monitoring and reporting application. The audience includes citizens, students, cleanup teams, researchers, partner organizations, and operators who need to understand coastal-waste evidence quickly.

The light visual direction is **Coastal Field Station**: sunlit, calm, capable, and evidence-led. The default dark mode is a focused deep-navy operations environment rather than pure black. Both modes share emerald primary actions, restrained yellow highlights, consistent geometry, and the same information hierarchy.

Public pages may use larger photography and more generous rhythm. Operational screens should be denser but still bright, highly legible, and composed from the same tokens. White panels, subtle green-gray borders, and low-chroma shadows replace dark control-room surfaces.

Prefer:

- Strong information hierarchy and short labels.
- Authentic coastal, drone, cleanup, and evidence photography.
- Clear map controls, status chips, and form states.
- Rounded but disciplined geometry.
- Motion that supports orientation, with reduced-motion fallbacks.

Avoid:

- Pure-black page backgrounds; use the documented deep navy in dark mode.
- Low-contrast gray text on pale surfaces.
- Excessive yellow, neon glow, glass effects, or oversized shadows.
- Rewriting established product language during visual work.
- Introducing decorative UI that competes with evidence imagery and map data.
